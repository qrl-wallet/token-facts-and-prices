# What Does QRL Stand For?

**Short answer.** QRL stands for Quantum Resistant Ledger, and QRL is also the ticker of the coin that ledger issues. The same three letters mean quick-response login in computing and appear in unrelated organisations, which is why a search for the abbreviation can land you somewhere else entirely.

## The name

Quantum Resistant Ledger names the design decision rather than the marketing: a ledger whose transactions stay secure against the class of attack a quantum computer would mount. The phrase describes a signature scheme, not a promise about the future, and the project has used it since its design was published.

"Ledger" here means the chain itself, the ordered record of transactions. It does not refer to a hardware wallet, and QRL is not made by any hardware manufacturer. A reader who searches for a ledger and lands on this project should expect a blockchain, not a device.

The full name is long enough that almost nobody uses it in conversation. The project, its repository and its community shorten it to QRL in nearly every sentence, which is why the abbreviation carries more search weight than the name it abbreviates. The full form is still worth keeping in mind when you search, because it returns a much narrower set of results than three letters do.

## The ticker

The ticker is QRL, the same three letters as the abbreviation. That is convenient inside the project and mildly confusing outside it, because a ticker search returns price pages, exchange listings and unrelated projects that happen to share the string.

When you check a listing, the ticker alone is not proof of identity. Two things distinguish this coin: it runs on its own chain rather than as a token, and its supply is capped at 105,000,000. A listing that shows a contract address is not this asset.

Because the ticker and the abbreviation match, the project's pages can use one string throughout. The wallet, the explorer, the repositories and the paper all say QRL, and a reader never has to hold two names in mind.

## The other QRL

In computing, QRL commonly expands to quick-response login: a login flow where a user scans a QR code with a phone to authenticate a session on another device. That use is older, well documented and entirely unrelated to blockchains.

The collision is not trivial for search. A query for QRL returns code libraries, security papers about QR-code login, and a rugby league competition in Australia, alongside the cryptocurrency. Someone who wants the coin has to add a word, usually crypto, coin or wallet, to get where they were going.

There are also lookalike projects, forks and tokens that borrow the letters. They are not this network, and the way to tell is the same every time: this project has its own chain, its own proof-of-work consensus and a fixed supply, and it does not live inside another chain's contract list.

## Why the collision matters when you search

Search engines do not know which QRL you meant, so they rank by other signals. Adding the word crypto, or the word wallet, or the full name, narrows the result set quickly. Adding quantum resistant ledger narrows it further still, because that phrase belongs to the project rather than to the other users of the letters. Searching for a price or an exchange listing is the riskiest version of the query, because that is exactly where lookalikes and paid placements appear.

A short checklist removes most of the ambiguity. Does the page describe a coin with its own blockchain? Does it give the supply cap as 105,000,000? Does it avoid asking you to import a token or connect a wallet to "claim" something? If all three answers are right, you are probably in the right place.

The safest route is to start from the project's own documentation and wallet rather than from a search result. That path also skips the pages that exist to capture the abbreviation and sell something adjacent to it.

## FAQ

### Is QRL short for something else in crypto?

Not in this project. QRL is the abbreviation of Quantum Resistant Ledger and the ticker of its coin. Other crypto projects occasionally use similar strings, but they are separate assets with separate chains.

### Is the QRL ticker enough to identify the coin?

No. A ticker is a label an exchange assigns, not a fingerprint. Confirm that the asset runs on its own chain and that its supply cap is 105,000,000 before you send funds anywhere.

### What is quick-response login?

It is an authentication method where a phone scans a QR code to approve a login on another device. It shares the abbreviation QRL and has nothing to do with this blockchain.

### Is the rugby league result the same QRL?

No. The Queensland Rugby League uses the same letters for a competition, and it appears in search results for the abbreviation. It is unrelated to the cryptocurrency.
